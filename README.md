# SRELO-2026

Project Category Code: CMPS (ai)

Title: Developing an Adaptive Wearable AI System for Neurorehabilitation Using Rhythmic Auditory Stimulation
Presenter: Allie Giacobbe1
Mentor: Ashwin Mohan, Ph.D. Computer Science Faculty, IMSA

Abstract/Project Intention:
Stroke survivors and individuals with Parkinson's disease (PD) commonly experience gait impairments that reduce mobility, independence, and quality of life. Although Rhythmic Auditory Stimulation (RAS) improves gait characteristics such as cadence, symmetry, and walking speed, few wearable systems integrate Artificial Intelligence (AI) to adapt RAS in real time. This study developed a low-cost AI-driven RAS system using three Micro:bits. Two ankle-mounted devices use machine learning (ML) to classify walking speed into one of eleven gait categories, while a third Micro:bit processes bilateral gait data to generate adaptive metronomic cadence outputs and calculate a Symmetry Index (SI) from left and right step intervals. RAS is activated when gait asymmetry exceeds a clinically established threshold. Preliminary testing showed that the AI model distinguished multiple walking speed conditions and generated cadence outputs that increased linearly with walking speed. Simulated asymmetric gait trials produced elevated SI values and successfully triggered RAS activation. These results demonstrate the feasibility of combining AI and RAS in an affordable wearable system for adaptive gait assistance. Future work will further validate the system and evaluate its effectiveness in neurorehabilitation.
